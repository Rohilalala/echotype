Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything that runs on your Mac is free during the beta and stays free after it. Pro, later, adds cloud features like meeting summaries.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download Echotype and its models the first time. After that, dictation works offline.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app with a text field: Mail, Slack, Notes, Messages, Google Docs in your browser, code editors, terminals. Text goes wherever your cursor is.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

No. Echotype runs its speech and cleanup models on the Mac itself, and that needs Apple silicon: any Mac with an M1 chip or later.

What happens to my recordings?

They stay on your Mac. History keeps recent dictations so you can re-run them, and meeting audio is deleted automatically after the time you choose. Nothing is uploaded.

When is Windows coming?

We're working on it. Join the waitlist above and we'll send you one email when it's ready.
