Last updated 12 October 2026.

The service

Echotype Cloud transcribes audio you send from the Echotype app and cleans up the resulting text. It is an optional, paid add-on to a free, open-source app. The app itself is licensed separately under the MIT licence.

Your account

You sign in with an email address you control. Your API key works like a password: keep it private. If you think it has leaked, sign in again for a new key and tell us so we can revoke the old one.

Plans and limits

The Free plan includes 2,000 transcribed words per week, resetting every Monday at 00:00 UTC, and up to 2 hours of audio per week. Pro has no word limit and a fair-use limit of 40 hours of audio per calendar month. When a limit is reached, requests are refused until it resets. Every request is limited to 25 MB of audio.

Payment

Pro costs $6 per month or $60 per year, billed in advance by Stripe. You can cancel at any time from the billing portal; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for and is not renewed after that. If a payment fails, the account returns to the Free plan until it succeeds.

Acceptable use

Do not use the service to break the law, to send audio you have no right to send, or to resell access. Do not try to get around the limits, for example with many accounts. We may suspend accounts that do.

No warranty

Transcription is automatic and can be wrong. The service is provided as is, without warranties of any kind, and we are not liable for losses that come from using it, to the extent the law allows. We may change these terms or the prices; changes to prices apply from your next billing period, and we will tell you by email first.
