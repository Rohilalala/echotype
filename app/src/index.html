Undo cleanupCopies your last dictation as you said it, before cleanup changed it. Undo in the app, then paste.

Transforms

Saved rewrites. Select text in any app and press a transform’s shortcut: your cleanup model rewrites it in place, as Command Mode would. On text you can’t edit, the result shows on the pill.
